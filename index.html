Questions answered

Frequently asked questions.

Is DOZLY a mobile application?

No. DOZLY is a digital student workspace built in Notion. You can access it through Notion's supported web and mobile applications.

Do I need a Notion account?

Yes. You need a Notion account to duplicate and use your DOZLY workspace. Notion offers a free plan.

Can I customise DOZLY?

Yes. You can adapt the workspace to your subjects, study routine, notes and personal preferences.

Is DOZLY suitable for different students?

DOZLY is designed as a flexible workspace for students who want to organise their academic responsibilities and learning resources.

Is this a recurring subscription?

DOZLY is sold as a one-time digital product purchase. Any separate Notion plan or service charges are subject to their respective providers.

How do I receive the product?

After purchasing through Gumroad, follow the instructions provided on the product page to access your digital workspace.
